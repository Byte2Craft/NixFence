# Byte2craft / NixFence brand use (proposed policy)

The Apache-2.0 license grants rights to the Community source code, **not a general right to use Byte2craft or NixFence names and logos as your own product brand**.

You may accurately identify the original project and state that a fork is based on NixFence. Clearly distinguish unaffiliated forks, products and services and do not imply Byte2craft endorsement.

Commercial brand licensing, uses in product names and third-party co-branding require separate permission from the mark holder. This draft policy is not a substitute for a professional trademark review; verify owner identity, trademark availability, jurisdictional rights and publication authority before claiming exclusive marks.
